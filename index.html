FATE turns failed agent trajectories into verifier-filtered repair supervision.

Abstract

Tool-using language agents can fail through complete trajectories rather than only final responses. FATE converts verifier-scored failures into repair supervision by having the current policy propose repairs, re-scoring those repairs across multiple objectives, and selecting Pareto-balanced targets for policy refinement.

Method

The pipeline first collects failed trajectories from the current policy, then asks the same policy to generate repair candidates. Each repair is scored for security, task utility, over-refusal control, and trajectory control. Feasible Pareto-front candidates become replay targets for SFT and PFPO updates.
